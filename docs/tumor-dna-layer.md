    
# Tumor DNA Layer in Thoracic Surgery Cohort

Pathology reports often contain detailed molecular and tumor DNA information, but this information is not always systematically integrated into clinical follow-up, outcome analysis, or research pipelines.

In this cohort project, the tumor DNA layer is considered one of the most valuable hidden dimensions of the dataset.

## Why it matters

- Molecular tumor information may explain differences in survival, recurrence, therapy response, and postoperative risk.
- Classical clinical variables alone may not capture the biological behavior of the disease.
- Combining clinical, pathological, radiological, and molecular information may create a much deeper understanding of each patient’s disease trajectory.

## Project direction

The aim is not only to collect tumor DNA information, but to connect it with:

- preoperative status
- tumor staging
- operative details
- postoperative complications
- adjuvant therapy
- recurrence
- long-term survival

## Core principle

Clinical data shows what happened.

Molecular data may help explain why it happened.
